## Description
This refrigerator ice bucket is a genuine replacement component designed to store ice produced by the icemaker before dispensing. Compatible with a wide range of models from Whirlpool, KitchenAid, Kenmore, Maytag, and Amana, it is constructed from durable plastic to withstand regular use.
