## Description
This is a manufacturer-made crisper drawer for your refrigerator. Crisper drawers are used to store fruits and vegetables at different humidity levels than the rest of your fridge. If your current crisper drawer is cracked or broken, it will need replacing immediately to ensure your food lasts as long as possible. This crisper drawer is made of clear and frosted plastic, and measures approximately 16.5" x 16.3" x 9.05" (Width x Depth x Height). This crisper drawer is sold individually and does not come with any other drawers or parts.
