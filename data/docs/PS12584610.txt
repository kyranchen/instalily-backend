## Description
This ice maker is a genuine OEM replacement part designed for use in refrigerators. The outer housing is made of highly durable plastic and measures approximately 13 inches by 5.5 inches. The assembly includes the icemaker unit, water inlet valve, ice mold, ice bin, thermostat, and wire connector. If any one of the parts in the unit fails, you will need to replace the entire assembly. The ice maker freezes water into ice cubes and ejects them into the ice bucket where they can be easily retrieved when needed. Over time the ice maker may become faulty due to normal wear and tear, damage, or a build-up of ice. The signs of a faulty assembly include difficulties making or dispensing ice, loud grinding or scraping noises, and large blocks of ice in the ice mold or tray.
