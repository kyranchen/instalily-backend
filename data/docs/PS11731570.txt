## Description
This dishwasher detergent dispenser is a genuine OEM replacement part designed for select models from Whirlpool, KitchenAid, Maytag, Amana, and other affiliated brands. Mounted on the inner door panel, it features separate, covered compartments for detergent and rinse aid, ensuring precise release during the wash cycle. The spring-loaded mechanism is engineered to dispense cleaning agents at optimal times, enhancing overall wash performance. Constructed from heat-resistant, high-grade materials, this dispenser offers long-term durability and reliability. It replaces older components such as W10620296 and W10620298.
